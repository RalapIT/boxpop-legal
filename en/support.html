Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos and other media processed?

Photos, videos, and Live Photos are processed on your device. Boxpop does not upload your original media or exported creations to a server.

Why does Boxpop need photo access?

Boxpop uses photo access when you choose media for a Zine or another visual creation, and when you save an export.

What can I do if an export fails?

Check that the selected media is still available. For larger projects, try exporting fewer pages at a time. Also check that Boxpop is allowed to add to Photos in iOS Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Boxpop has no accounts, ads, or cloud sync. Membership purchases are available through the App Store. Analytics and performance data are collected only after you consent.

How do I change the language?

Open the sidebar, choose Settings, then Language. Choose English or 简体中文. The interface updates immediately and remembers your choice. iOS manages the language of system permission prompts and keyboards.
